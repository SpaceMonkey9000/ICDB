# ICDB PySide6 — UI Revision 8: original Light and Dark restored

- `Light` restores ICDB Revision 6's light palette (#F0F0F0 window, white fields, #E6E6E6 buttons).
- `Dark` restores ICDB Revision 6's dark graphite palette (#30343B window, #25282E fields, #404650 buttons).
- `Graphite`, `Slate`, `Windows classic`, and `Warm light` remain unchanged.
- Dark pinout inversion remains independently selectable.
- Existing `appearance/theme=dark` now selects restored ICDB Dark rather than being redirected to Graphite.
- No database, editor, or grouping changes.
